About Velori Analytics

Built to elevate community sports through powerful data insights.

Our Mission

Velori Analytics was created to bring professional-level analytics to community and recreational sports leagues. We believe that every player and team deserves access to performance insights.

Our platform provides easy-to-use dashboards, league comparisons, and AI-powered scouting reports to help teams grow and compete at a higher level.

Why Velori?

We combine modern technology with a passion for sports to create a platform that is simple, powerful, and built for real competition.
