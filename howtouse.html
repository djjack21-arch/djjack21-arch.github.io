How to Use Velori Analytics

Learn how to set up your league, track performance, and maximize insights.

Getting Started

Create an account, select your league, and add players to begin tracking performance. Velori Analytics makes it easy to set up your dashboard in just a few minutes.

  1. Sign up and log in.
  2. Create or join a league.
  3. Add teams and players.

Using Key Features

  • Player Dashboards: View individual statistics and performance trends.
  • League Insights: Compare teams and analyze standings.
  • AI Scouting Reports: Generate automatic summaries for matchups.

Tips & Best Practices

  • Update stats consistently after each game.
  • Review trends weekly to improve team strategy.
  • Share dashboards with players to increase engagement.
